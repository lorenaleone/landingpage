About us

Born in Santo Amaro to show the park from within.

Brisa Azul Viagens was founded in 2016 by Marina Ribeiro, daughter of a fisherman from Rio Preguiças, with a simple idea: the best spots in Lençóis Maranhenses aren't on the common routes.

Each Brisa Azul circuit departs from the center of Santo Amaro and reaches lagoons most tourists never see — unpaved trails, lagoons without selfie sticks, dunes where the only sound is the wind.

Today there are six circuits, a team of trained native guides, and over 1,800 travelers who returned home with tired legs and full memories.

Tours

Six routes. One starting point: Santo Amaro.

Each circuit reaches a different part of the park. You can combine more than one — we'll plan the itinerary together.

Most popular Departs 8am

Emendadas Circuit

The most complete. The Emendadas Lagoons form a water mirror connecting two lagoons — the aerial view is the photo everyone wants.

  • Stop at 3 lagoons (Emendadas, Preta and Manga)
  • Exclusive access via 4×4 trail
  • Panoramic sunset on the dunes
Departs 7:30am

Américas Circuit

The most remote. Turquoise-blue lagoons in the least visited area of the park, with crystal-clear water and almost no other tourists.

  • Clearest lagoon in Santo Amaro
  • Regional lunch included
  • Maximum 6 people per departure
Departs 2pm

Andorinhas Circuit

The afternoon circuit. Named after the flocks of swallows that cross the sky at dusk, when the light turns orange and the dune becomes a stage.

  • Ideal timing for the sunset
  • Easy trail, accessible for all ages
  • Perfect to combine with another circuit
Departs 8am

Betânia Circuit

Nature and culture: the route passes through the Betânia community, with a stop at the lagoon and a conversation with the oasis residents.

  • Visit to the Betânia quilombola community
  • Farm-style breakfast included
  • Great for groups with children
Departs 8am

Ponta Verde Circuit

The circuit of surprises. Ponta Verde changes its appearance between June and September — during high water, it looks like another planet.

  • Double view: lagoon on one side, dune on the other
  • 4×4 quicksand crossing
  • One of the most photographed spots in Santo Amaro
Departs 8am

Rancharia Circuit

The longest and deepest into the park. For those who truly want to go off the map — no other operator in Santo Amaro covers this stretch.

  • 4 lagoons in sequence
  • Lunch at the oasis with a local family
  • Maximum 6 people, booking 48h in advance
